Malocclusion categories included in the research

Crossbite

Abnormal relationship where upper teeth may bite inside lower teeth.

Open Bite

Upper and lower teeth fail to make normal contact in part of the bite.

Crowding

Limited dental arch space may cause displacement or overlapping of teeth.

Spacing

Visible or excessive spaces occur between teeth.
