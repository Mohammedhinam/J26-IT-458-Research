## Proposed A safeguards to freeze BEFORE new qualification

Retain the prior forward budgets rather than retuning them: **mean+0.01 ≤0.50; P99.9 enclosing upper bound+0.01 ≤1.00; maximum+0.01 ≤2.00 ΔE76**, measured per image in common D50 Lab at original geometry before resize. The reserve is the previously documented generated-reference numerical allowance, not a universal uncertainty guarantee. All conditions are ANDed, no masking by labels/teeth/brightness and no per-image exemptions.

Replace zero-gamut-loss with this **candidate engineering loss budget**, for qualification/owner decision only:

- Define extended normalized sRGB excursion `e(p)=max_channels(max(0,-r,r-1))`, before clamping. Retain numerical reporting epsilon **1e-6**.
- Count `C=count(e>1e-6)` must satisfy **`C ≤ floor(N/1000)` AND `C/N ≤0.001` (0.10%)**. The integer count limit is resolution-scaled, not an image-specific count chosen from a failed photograph; report actual count and exact fraction.
- **Maximum excursion ≤`2/255` =0.00784313725490196** normalized encoded sRGB: at most two destination eight-bit code levels beyond either boundary. Any pixel exceeding this cap rejects, regardless of how few pixels are affected or how low the mean ΔE is.
- Preserve raw below-epsilon excursion reporting, separate channel/unique-pixel counts and reverse-error statistics. Do not describe permitted clipping as rounding only. The old reverse-source-code limit 2 and this proposed destination-excursion budget are different quantities, not interchangeable tolerances.
- Every malformed/unsupported/ambiguous/non-finite/incomplete transform, geometry/hash/metadata/authorization/isolation failure rejects. A failure stops the package attempt, without dropping/replacing an image or widening limits.

**Independent rationale versus project judgment:** the excursion unit is anchored to destination encoding precision; the count/fraction budget bounds its spatial extent, and separate Lab tail/maximum gates prevent average colour differences concealing isolated severe errors. The choices “two code levels” and “one pixel in a thousand” are **explicit project engineering-risk budgets**, not scientifically established dental tolerances or numbers mandated by ICC/CIE. There is no honest universal citation proving them safe. [CIE image-difference guidance](https://cie.co.at/publications/methods-evaluating-colour-differences-images) supports measuring image differences under comparable conditions, not assigning these clinical limits. Their defensibility requires transparent non-clinical scope, independent synthetic qualification, prospective freezing and honest loss reporting.

The saved wide-gamut fixture demonstrates detection of substantial loss (2/3 pixels, excursion 1.291863441, maximum ΔE76 26.658965929); the saved shadow fixture demonstrates quantisation-only reverse error 4 with maximum forward ΔE76 0.227673421 and no gamut flags. Neither tests the **new small-excursion acceptance boundaries**. Existing 40 tests therefore do not validate or activate A.

Its cached numbers fall within these candidate budgets, but that is **retrospective compatibility, not independent justification or a new successful run**.
